FAQs

Questions?
We've got you.

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

Do I need an appointment? +

Appointments are recommended, especially during evenings, weekends and wedding season. Walk-ins are also welcome depending on stylist availability.

Can I book through WhatsApp? +

Yes. Simply click the WhatsApp booking button, share your service and preferred time, and our team will confirm your appointment.

Are the prices fixed? +

The prices shown are starting prices. Some services may vary based on hair length, product selection, treatment requirements or the complexity of the service. We always explain the final price before starting.

Do you accept UPI and cards? +

Yes. You can pay using UPI, card or cash at the salon.

Can I change or cancel my appointment? +

Yes. Please contact us on WhatsApp as early as possible if you need to reschedule or cancel.

Do you provide bridal consultations? +

Yes. We recommend booking a bridal consultation before your wedding so we can understand your outfit, functions, preferred makeup style and hair requirements.
